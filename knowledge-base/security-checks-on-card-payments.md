# Security checks on card payments

To protect your money, we check card payments for signs of fraud. If a payment looks unusual, we may decline it and stop your card until we have checked with you. In the app, the decline shows as **blocked for a security check**.

## What to do

- Contact us. Only our team can check the payment and unblock your card. If you ask the assistant, it passes your question to our team, and someone will follow up with you. See [How to contact support](contact-support.md).
- If you made the payment, tell us. Once we have confirmed it was you, we can unblock your card and you can try the payment again.
- If you did not make the payment, tell us straight away.

For your security, we do not explain which checks a payment triggered.

## Staying safe

We will never:

- ask for your full card number, your PIN, your app passcode or a one-time code,
- ask you to move money to a "safe account",
- ask you to install software so we can see your screen.

If someone contacts you claiming to be from us and asks for any of these, stop, hang up, and contact us using the details in the app or on the back of your card.

## What stays the same

A security check only affects card payments. Your standing orders, scheduled payments and Direct Debits keep working.
