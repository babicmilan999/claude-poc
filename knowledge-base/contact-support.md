# How to contact support

## Ask the assistant first

The assistant in the app can answer most questions about cards and payments straight away. It can see your cards, recent declined payments, card controls, standing orders, scheduled payments, Direct Debits and balance, and explain what happened.

The assistant can only look at your information. It cannot make changes for you, such as unfreezing a card or sending a payment. Instead it tells you how to do it yourself in the app.

## Talking to a person

You can ask to talk to a person at any time. Just say so, for example "I want to talk to someone".

The assistant also passes your question to our team when:

- it is about suspected fraud, or payments you don't recognise,
- you want to make a complaint,
- you are going through a difficult time, for example a bereavement, and need extra support,
- only our team can fix the problem, for example a card blocked for a security check, or a refund,
- it is not sure it understands the problem, or its answer did not help.

When this happens, it creates a case for our team with your conversation and what it has already found, so you don't have to explain everything again. The assistant tells you that someone will follow up.

## Other ways to contact us

You can also call us on the number on the back of your card, or the number shown in the app.

## Keeping you safe

We will never ask for your full card number, your PIN, your app passcode or a one-time code, and we will never ask you to move money to a "safe account". See [Security checks on card payments](security-checks-on-card-payments.md).
