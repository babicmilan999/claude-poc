# Lost, stolen and blocked cards

## If your card is lost or stolen

If you think you have just mislaid your card, you can [freeze it](freeze-and-unfreeze-your-card.md) in the app while you look for it, and unfreeze it if you find it.

If your card is lost for good or has been stolen, contact us straight away so we can block it and send you a new one. See [How to contact support](contact-support.md). A card that has been reported lost or stolen stays blocked for good, even if you find it later: destroy it and use your replacement card.

## What a blocked card means

A blocked card declines every payment. In the app, the card is marked **Blocked**. A card can be blocked for two reasons:

- It was reported lost or stolen.
- We stopped it for a security check, because a payment looked unusual. See [Security checks on card payments](security-checks-on-card-payments.md).

Unlike a frozen card, you cannot unblock a card yourself in the app. Our team needs to help. If you ask the assistant about a blocked card, it passes your question to our team with the details, so you do not have to explain it again.

## If you spot payments you don't recognise

Freeze your card in the app and contact us straight away. Do not wait for your statement.

## What stays the same

A blocked card only affects that card. Your account, your other cards, standing orders, scheduled payments and Direct Debits keep working.
