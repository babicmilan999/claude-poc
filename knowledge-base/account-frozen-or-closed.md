# When your account is frozen or closed

Your account's status is shown in the app. Most accounts are **Active**. If your account is **Frozen** or **Closed**, payments from it stop working.

## What stops working

While your account is frozen or closed:

- card payments with any card on the account are declined,
- standing orders and scheduled payments are not sent,
- Direct Debits cannot be collected.

## Why an account can be frozen or closed

We may freeze an account while we check something on it, for example to protect you if we think someone else may have access to it, or when we need information from you. An account can be closed at your request, or by us in line with your account terms.

Account freezes are different from [freezing your card](freeze-and-unfreeze-your-card.md). You can freeze and unfreeze a card yourself, but only our team can help with an account that is frozen or closed.

## What to do

Contact us. If you ask the assistant, it passes your question to our team with the details. See [How to contact support](contact-support.md).

If you have Direct Debits or regular payments with companies, let them know that payments from this account may not reach them, so they can agree another way to pay with you.
