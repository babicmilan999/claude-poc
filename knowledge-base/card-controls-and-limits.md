# Card controls and limits

Card controls let you decide where and how your card can be used. You manage them in the app: go to **Cards**, choose the card and open **Card controls**. Changes apply straight away.

## Contactless limit

Your contactless limit is the largest single contactless payment your card will make. A contactless payment above it is declined.

- To pay more than your limit, insert your card and enter your PIN. Chip and PIN payments are not affected by the contactless limit.
- You can see your current contactless limit in **Card controls**, and change it there.

## Blocked merchant types

You can block your card from being used with certain types of merchant, for example gambling. Payments to a blocked type of merchant are declined.

- To see which types are blocked, open **Card controls**, then **Blocked merchant types**.
- To allow a type again, remove it from the list.

Every shop and website is given a merchant type by its own bank, so a payment can be blocked even if the shop does not look like that type to you. If a payment was declined because of a merchant type you did not expect, the decline in the app shows it.

## Online payments

You can turn off online payments. While they are off, payments on websites, in apps and over the phone are declined. Payments in shops (chip and PIN or contactless) still work.

To turn online payments back on, open **Card controls** and turn on **Online payments**.

## Card controls do not affect your account payments

Standing orders, scheduled payments and Direct Debits are not card payments, so card controls do not apply to them. For limits on payments you send from your account, see [Daily payment limit](daily-payment-limit.md).
