# Daily payment limit

To protect your account, there is a limit on the total amount you can send from your account in payments each day.

## What counts towards the limit

All payments you send from your account on the same day count together:

- payments and transfers you make in the app,
- standing orders,
- scheduled payments.

Card payments and Direct Debits do not count towards this limit. Card payments are covered by your [card controls](card-controls-and-limits.md), and Direct Debits are collected by the company you pay.

## If a payment goes over the limit

The payment is not sent and the money stays in your account. For a standing order or scheduled payment, the app shows **over your daily payment limit** as the reason. This often happens when several large payments, such as rent and a savings transfer, are due on the same day.

## What you can do

- Send the payment on another day.
- Move one of your regular payments to a different date, so they do not all go out together.
- If you regularly need to send more in a day, contact us. See [How to contact support](contact-support.md).

See also [Why a scheduled payment failed](why-a-scheduled-payment-failed.md).
