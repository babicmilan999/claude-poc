# Why a scheduled payment failed

If a standing order or scheduled payment did not go out, the app shows why. Go to **Payments**, then **Scheduled**, choose the payment and look at **Last payment**. You can also ask the assistant "why didn't my payment go out?".

When a payment fails, the money stays in your account and the person or company you were paying does not receive it. You may need to let them know.

## Not enough money in your account

On the payment date, the payment was more than your available balance. See [Your balance and available funds](balance-and-available-funds.md).

What to do: add money to your account, then send the amount as a one-off payment if it is still owed. Check that your next payment date has enough money too.

## Over your daily payment limit

Payments sent from your account on the same day count towards a daily limit. If the payment would have taken you over it, it was not sent. See [Daily payment limit](daily-payment-limit.md).

What to do: send the payment on another day, or move other payments to a different day.

## The payee's details are wrong

The receiving bank could not find an account with the sort code and account number you gave.

What to do: check the details with the person or company you are paying, update the payee in the app, and send the payment again.

## The payee's account is closed

The receiving bank told us the account you are paying into is closed, so the payment could not be delivered.

What to do: ask the person or company you are paying for their new account details. Our team can help with the payment that failed: ask the assistant, which will pass your question to our team, or see [How to contact support](contact-support.md).

## Your account is not active

Payments cannot be sent from an account that is frozen or closed. Our team needs to help with this. See [When your account is frozen or closed](account-frozen-or-closed.md).

## The payment shows as skipped

A skipped payment was not attempted on that date, so no money left your account. Check whether the payment is paused, and ask the assistant if you are not sure why.
