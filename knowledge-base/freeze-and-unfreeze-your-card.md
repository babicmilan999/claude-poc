# Freeze and unfreeze your card

Freezing your card stops it from being used, without cancelling it. It is useful if you have mislaid your card and think you will find it, or if you want to stop spending for a while.

## What happens while your card is frozen

- Every card payment is declined: in shops, contactless, online and in apps.
- The card itself is not cancelled. Your card number, PIN and expiry date stay the same.
- Your account keeps working as usual. Standing orders, scheduled payments and Direct Debits are not affected, because they are not card payments.

## How to freeze your card

1. In the app, go to **Cards**.
2. Choose the card.
3. Turn on **Freeze card**.

## How to unfreeze your card

1. In the app, go to **Cards**.
2. Choose the card. A frozen card is marked **Frozen**.
3. Turn off **Freeze card**.

The card works again straight away. If a payment was declined while the card was frozen, the shop will not retry it: try the payment again.

## "I don't remember freezing my card"

Only someone using your app can freeze a card, so it is usually a forgotten tap. If you are sure nobody froze it and you think someone else may have access to your app, tell us straight away. See [How to contact support](contact-support.md).

## Frozen or blocked?

A frozen card is one you can unfreeze yourself. A **blocked** card has been stopped because it was reported lost or stolen, or for a security check, and only our team can help with it. See [Lost, stolen and blocked cards](lost-stolen-or-blocked-cards.md).
