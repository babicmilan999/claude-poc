# How Direct Debits and mandates work

A Direct Debit lets a company or organisation (for example your energy supplier, gym, phone company or letting agent) collect money from your account. You give them permission once, and they collect each payment when it is due. The amount and date can change, but the company must tell you in advance.

## Mandates

Your permission is called a **Direct Debit instruction**, or **mandate**. The company sets it up with the details you give them. Each mandate is between you and one company, with its own reference.

In the app, go to **Payments**, then **Direct Debits**, to see:

- each mandate, the company and the reference,
- its status: **Active**, **Cancelled** or **Dormant**,
- recent collections and whether each one was collected, is pending or failed.

A mandate becomes **dormant** if the company has not collected anything for a long time. The company may need to set it up again before collecting.

## Cancelling a Direct Debit

You can cancel a Direct Debit in the app at any time. Once it is cancelled, the company cannot collect any more payments with it, including payments you still owe them. Before you cancel, check that you no longer need it: cancelling the wrong mandate is a common reason rent or bills don't get paid.

Cancelling a Direct Debit does not cancel your contract with the company. If you still owe them money, arrange another way to pay.

A company can also cancel a mandate on its side, for example when you end your contract with them.

## When a collection fails

If a collection fails, no money leaves your account and the company is told it was not paid. The company decides what happens next: it may try again, or ask you to pay another way. See [Why a Direct Debit failed](why-a-direct-debit-failed.md).

## Direct Debit or standing order?

With a Direct Debit, the company collects the money. With a standing order, you send it. See [How standing orders and scheduled payments work](how-scheduled-payments-work.md).

## The Direct Debit Guarantee

Direct Debits are protected by the Direct Debit Guarantee. If an error is made in a payment by the company or by us, you are entitled to a refund of the amount paid. Refund claims are handled by our team: see [How to contact support](contact-support.md).
