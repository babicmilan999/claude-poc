# Fees and charges

## Where to see fees

Any fee we charge appears in your transactions in the app as its own transaction, marked as a fee, with a description of what it is for.

The fees that apply to your account are listed in your account terms.

## Fees charged by other companies

A company you pay may charge its own fees, for example a late payment fee when a Direct Debit or standing order to them fails. Those fees are set by the company, not by us, and you need to raise them with the company.

Shops and websites may also charge a fee for paying by card. That fee is part of the payment you make to them.

## Payments in other currencies

If you pay in a currency other than your account's currency, the payment is converted. Check your account terms for any fees on payments abroad or in other currencies.

## If you think a fee is wrong

Contact us and our team will look into it. The assistant can show you your transactions, but it cannot refund a fee: a refund is decided by our team. If you ask the assistant about a refund, it passes your question to our team. See [How to contact support](contact-support.md).
