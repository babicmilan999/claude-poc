# Expired cards and replacement cards

Every card has an expiry date (month and year) printed on it. The card works until the last day of that month. After that, every payment with it is declined.

## Using your new card

- Your replacement card is usually sent before your old card expires. Start using it as soon as it arrives.
- Your replacement card has a new expiry date and security code. Some shops and services that store your card details (subscriptions, online shops, apps) need you to update them.
- In the app, go to **Cards** to see each card and its status. An expired card is marked **Expired**.

## A payment was declined because my card expired

Pay with your new card instead. If a subscription or regular card payment failed, update the card details with that company.

## I haven't received my new card

Contact us and we will check what happened and arrange a new card. See [How to contact support](contact-support.md).

## Card payments and account payments

An expired card only affects card payments. Standing orders, scheduled payments and Direct Debits come from your account, not your card, so they keep working.
