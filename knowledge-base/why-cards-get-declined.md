# Why cards get declined

When a card payment is declined, the shop or website usually only says "declined". The reason is shown in the app: go to **Cards**, choose the card and look at the payment under **Recent declines**. You can also ask the assistant "why was my card declined?".

These are the reasons a card payment can be declined, and what you can do about each one.

## Your card is frozen

You (or someone with access to your app) froze the card. Frozen cards decline every payment until you unfreeze them. You can unfreeze the card yourself in the app in a few seconds. See [Freeze and unfreeze your card](freeze-and-unfreeze-your-card.md).

## Not enough money in your account

The payment was more than your available balance. Your available balance is your balance plus any overdraft you have, minus card payments that are still pending. See [Your balance and available funds](balance-and-available-funds.md).

## Your card has expired

A card stops working after the last day of the month shown on it. Use your new card instead. See [Expired cards and replacement cards](expired-cards-and-replacements.md).

## The payment was over your contactless limit

Contactless payments above your contactless limit are declined. Insert your card and enter your PIN instead. See [Card controls and limits](card-controls-and-limits.md).

## You blocked this type of shop

If you blocked a type of merchant in your card controls (for example gambling), payments to that type of merchant are declined. See [Card controls and limits](card-controls-and-limits.md).

## Online payments are turned off

If you turned off online payments for your card, payments on websites and in apps are declined, while payments in shops still work. See [Card controls and limits](card-controls-and-limits.md).

## Your card is blocked

A card is blocked when it has been reported lost or stolen, or when we have stopped it for a security check. You cannot unblock it yourself. See [Lost, stolen and blocked cards](lost-stolen-or-blocked-cards.md) and [Security checks on card payments](security-checks-on-card-payments.md).

## Your account is not active

If your account is frozen or closed, card payments from it are declined. Our team needs to help with this. See [When your account is frozen or closed](account-frozen-or-closed.md).

## Still not sure?

Ask the assistant in the app. It can see your recent declines and explain them. If the problem needs a person, it will pass your question to our team. See [How to contact support](contact-support.md).
