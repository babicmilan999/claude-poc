# Why a Direct Debit failed

If a company tells you a Direct Debit was not paid, you can see why in the app. Go to **Payments**, then **Direct Debits**, choose the company and look at its recent collections. You can also ask the assistant "why did my Direct Debit fail?".

When a collection fails, no money leaves your account. The company decides what happens next: it may try to collect again, or ask you to pay another way. It may also charge its own late fee; this is between you and the company.

## The Direct Debit was cancelled

The mandate was cancelled, so the company could not collect. This often happens when old Direct Debits are tidied up and the wrong one is cancelled.

What to do: contact the company and ask them to set up the Direct Debit again, and pay what you owe another way if they ask you to. Only the company can set up a new Direct Debit. See [How Direct Debits and mandates work](how-direct-debits-work.md).

## There is no Direct Debit set up

The company tried to collect, but we have no Direct Debit instruction from them for your account. The company may have used the wrong details, or the setup may not have finished.

What to do: contact the company and check the account details they have for you.

## Not enough money in your account

On the due date, the amount was more than your available balance. See [Your balance and available funds](balance-and-available-funds.md).

What to do: add money to your account and contact the company to arrange the payment. Ask them whether they will try to collect again.

## The company closed the Direct Debit on its side

The company told us the arrangement this Direct Debit belongs to has been closed on their side, so the collection failed. Our team needs to look into this with you: ask the assistant, which will pass your question to our team, or see [How to contact support](contact-support.md).

## Your account is closed

Direct Debits cannot be collected from a closed account. See [When your account is frozen or closed](account-frozen-or-closed.md).

## A payment you were expecting is not a Direct Debit

If you pay this company by standing order, see [Why a scheduled payment failed](why-a-scheduled-payment-failed.md) instead.
