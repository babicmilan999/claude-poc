# How standing orders and scheduled payments work

A **standing order** is a regular payment of a fixed amount that you set up to another account, for example rent to your landlord every month. A **scheduled payment** is a one-off payment you set up now to go out on a future date. Both are sent from your account by us, on your instruction.

You can see them in the app under **Payments**, then **Scheduled**.

## When they are paid

- Each payment has a **next payment date**. On that date we try to send the payment.
- A standing order can repeat weekly or monthly. After each payment the next date moves on.
- A one-off scheduled payment runs once, then it is marked **Completed**.

To be safe, make sure the money is in your account by the day before the payment date.

## What you can see in the app

For each standing order or scheduled payment the app shows:

- who it is paid to, the amount and the reference,
- how often it is paid and the next payment date,
- its status: **Active**, **Paused**, **Cancelled** or **Completed**,
- the result of the last payment: paid, failed or skipped, with the reason if it did not go out.

A paused standing order does not send payments until you resume it.

## When a payment does not go out

If a payment fails, the money stays in your account and the person or company you were paying does not receive it. The app shows the reason. See [Why a scheduled payment failed](why-a-scheduled-payment-failed.md).

## Standing order or Direct Debit?

They are often confused. With a standing order, **you** decide the amount and the date, and you send the money. With a Direct Debit, the **company** you pay collects the money from your account, and the amount can change. If a company takes the payment, it is a Direct Debit: see [How Direct Debits and mandates work](how-direct-debits-work.md).
